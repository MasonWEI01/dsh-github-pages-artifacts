01Foundations資訊系統基礎資訊系統基礎Foundations

The one distinction唯一的分辨點

Data are raw facts with no meaning on their own — a phone number, a height, an exam score, a list of everything sold today. Information is data processed into meaning that supports a decision — a class average, a pass rate, actual versus forecast sales.數據是本身沒有意義的原始事實——電話號碼、身高、考試分數、今日售出貨品清單。資訊是經過處理、能支持決策的有意義結果——班平均、及格率、實際與預測銷售額的比較。

Test: if a calculation across records was needed, it is information. If it can be read straight off a receipt, it is data.判斷法：需要跨紀錄計算才得出的，是資訊；能直接從收據讀出的，是數據。

A system has seven parts系統有七個部分

Input輸入→ Processes處理→ Output輸出↺ Feedback回饋
  • System, boundary (the functions inside), environment (external entities), inputs, processes, outputs, feedback.系統本身、邊界（界內功能）、環境（外部實體）、輸入、處理程序、輸出、回饋。
  • ATM example: input = card, PIN, amount; process = verify, debit, dispense; output = cash, updated balance, receipt; customers sit outside the boundary.自動櫃員機例子：輸入＝銀行卡、密碼、金額；處理＝核對、扣帳、出鈔；輸出＝現金、更新後餘額、收據；顧客在邊界之外。
  • Positive feedback raises motivation; negative feedback corrects activity that is not reaching the goal.正向回饋提升動機；負向回饋糾正未達目標的行為。
  • Why computers: speed, reliability, consistency, storage, communication.用電腦的理由：速度、可靠、一致、儲存、通訊。
Trap易錯位

IT is an enabler of business success, never a guarantee. Spending more on IT does not by itself produce results — the money has to go to the right kinds of IT.IT 只是成功的賦能者，不是保證。多花錢本身不會帶來成果——錢要花在對的 IT 上。

02Measuring IT value衡量 IT 價值衡量 IT 價值Measuring IT value

Efficiency 效率效率 Effectiveness 效能效能
Does the technology perform well? Easy to measure.問技術本身表現好不好。容易量度。 Does the system move the business toward its goals? Hard to measure.問系統有沒有把業務推向目標。難以量度。
Throughput · transaction speed · system availability · information accuracy · response time · web traffic吞吐量・交易速度・系統可用度・資訊準確度・回應時間・網站流量 Usability (clicks) · customer satisfaction · conversion rate · ROI / cost-benefit · break-even易用性（點擊次數）・客戶滿意度・轉換率・投資回報／成本效益・損益兩平
  • A business continuity (resiliency) plan responds in tiers: total loss at one end, then data backup, maintaining transaction integrity, and manual processing at the other.營運持續（韌性）計劃分層應對：一端是全面損失，另一端是資料備份、維持交易完整性、人工處理程序。
  • Canonical example: the CrowdStrike faulty Falcon Sensor update on 19 July 2024 that broke Microsoft Windows worldwide.經典例子：2024 年 7 月 19 日 CrowdStrike 的 Falcon Sensor 錯誤更新，令全球 Microsoft Windows 大規模故障。
One-liner一句話

eBay needs both: constant availability (efficiency) and buyers who actually complete the purchase (effectiveness).eBay 兩者都需要：持續可用（效率）與買家真的完成購買（效能）。

03The SDLC軟件開發生命週期軟件開發生命週期The SDLC

  • Software = instructions and data that make a computer work. Software development = the whole life of that software. Programming = writing code, and it is stage 4 only.軟件＝令電腦工作的指令與資料。軟件開發＝軟件的整個生命周期。程式編寫＝寫程式碼，只是第四階段。
1 Plan1 規劃→ 2 Requirements2 需求定義→ 3 Design3 系統設計→ 4 Development4 開發→ 5 Test5 測試整合→ 6 Deploy6 部署維護
Stage階段 Remember this要記住
1 Planning & requirement analysis規劃與需求分析 Feasibility study (economic, operational, technical, schedule, legal); project proposal; scope. The project plan must be updated in every later phase.可行性研究（經濟、運作、技術、時程、法律）；專案提案；範圍。專案計劃要在之後每個階段持續更新。
2 Defining requirements需求定義 Functional vs non-functional; elicitation by interview, workshop, survey, focus group, observation, document analysis.功能／非功能需求；透過訪談、工作坊、問卷、焦點小組、觀察、文件分析蒐集。
3 System design & analysis系統設計與分析 The Data Flow Diagram belongs here; SRS becomes the design specification.資料流程圖屬於這個階段；需求規格轉為設計規格。
4 Development開發 Programmers, compilers, debuggers — programming is stage 4.程式員、編譯器、除錯器——程式編寫＝第四階段。
5 Testing & integration測試與整合 Test plans, cases, bug reports; minimal testing still happens in every stage.測試計劃、案例、缺陷報告；每階段仍會做最基本的測試。
6 Deployment & maintenance部署與維護 Released in pilot phases, then patches and new releases driven by user feedback.分階段（試點）上線，之後依使用者回饋推出修補與新版。
Trap · ambiguity易錯位・含糊

Ambiguous requirements are the leading cause of system failure. “Easy to use”, “fast”, “large amounts of data” cannot be tested. “Respond within 2 seconds” and “support 100 concurrent users” can.需求含糊是系統失敗的首要原因。「易於使用」、「快」、「大量數據」無法驗證；「2 秒內回應」、「支援 100 個並行使用者」才可以。

04Security網絡安全網絡安全Security

  • A threat is an event that may break a system's security and cause loss. Hacking is the act; a hacker is the person; an intrusion is the attempt to get in.威脅是可能破壞系統安全並造成損失的事件；入侵行為是動作本身；駭客是人；入侵是嘗試闖入。

Ten threats, two families十種威脅，兩大家族

Malware — needs a program on your machine惡意軟件——需要程式在你機器上 Network & social — needs no local program網絡與社交——不需本地程式
Virus — spreads and infects, can corrupt or delete; harmful even when “harmless” because it eats memory and CPU time.
Trojan horse — hides inside a legitimate-looking program and opens a back door.
Spyware — key-loggers and screen capture, sends your data away.
Adware — unwanted advertisements and pop-ups.病毒——自我複製並感染，可破壞或刪除資料；即使「無害」也有害，因為佔用記憶體與 CPU 時間。
木馬——藏身於看似合法的程式，開啟後門。
間諜軟件——鍵盤記錄、螢幕截取，把資料送走。
廣告軟件——不受歡迎的廣告與彈窗。		 DDoS — flood the service from many sources until it cannot answer.
Sniffing — capture and read packets on a network.
Spamming — unsolicited mail whose links install malware.
Phishing — pretend to be a trusted entity to extract personal data.
Bandwidth piracy — free ride on someone else's network.DDoS——從多個來源灌流量，令服務無法回應。
竊聽——在網絡上截取並讀取封包。
垃圾郵件——大量未經請求的郵件，連結會安裝惡意程式。
釣魚——冒充可信機構騙取個人資料。
頻寬盜用——免費使用他人網絡。
  • CIA — confidentiality (no unauthorised disclosure), integrity (no unauthorised modification), availability (there when needed).CIA——機密性（不被未授權揭露）、完整性（不被未授權修改）、可用性（需要時可用）。
  • Controls: antivirus, encryption, access control (username/password, firewall between LAN and WAN, intrusion detection, DMZ), authentication (token, SMS one-time PIN, biometrics, digital certificate).防護措施：防毒、加密、存取控制（帳號密碼、LAN 與 WAN 之間的防火牆、入侵偵測、DMZ）、認證（權杖、短訊一次性密碼、生物特徵、數位證書）。
  • Public key encrypts, private key decrypts. A certificate authority is the trusted third party that vouches for the public key.公鑰加密、私鑰解密。憑證機構是為公鑰背書的可信第三方。
Trap · authentication ≠ authorisation易錯位・認證 ≠ 授權

Authentication asks “who are you?” and comes first. Authorisation asks “what may you do?” and comes second — a correct password proves identity, never permission.認證問「你是誰」，先發生；授權問「你可以做甚麼」，後發生——密碼正確只證明身份，不代表權限。

05Solvers and models優化求解求解器與模型Solvers and models

  • A solver allocates scarce resources (raw materials, machine time, people, money) to find the best outcome — highest profit, lowest cost, best quality.求解器分配稀缺資源（原料、機器時間、人力、金錢），找出最佳結果——最高利潤、最低成本、最佳品質。
Decision variables決策變數+ Constraints限制條件→ Objective目標函數
Piece部分 Excel nameExcel 名稱 Example例子
Decision variables決策變數 changing cells變動儲存格 units of each product to make每種產品的生產量
Constraints限制條件 constraint rows限制列 machine hours ≤ 8 per day; ball ≥ 40,000 per month; variables ≥ 0每日機器工時 ≤ 8；每月產量 ≥ 40,000；變數 ≥ 0
Objective目標函數 target cell目標儲存格 maximise profit; minimise cost or risk最大化利潤；最小化成本或風險
  • Everything must be a number — “a solver deals with numbers”. Five constraint kinds: general, bounds, policy, physical (non-negativity, balance), integer.一切都必須是數字——「求解器處理的是數字」。五類限制：一般、上下界、政策、物理（非負、平衡）、整數。
  • Three engines: Simplex LP, GRG Nonlinear, Evolutionary.三個引擎：Simplex LP、GRG Nonlinear、Evolutionary。
  • What-if / sensitivity analysis varies the inputs (growth, oil price, salaries) and asks which uncertainty drives the output most.情境／敏感度分析改變輸入（增長率、油價、薪金），問哪個不確定因素對輸出影響最大。
Trap易錯位

Profit is a goal; costs are a constraint. And non-negativity must be stated explicitly — the solver has no other way to know.利潤是目標；成本是限制。非負限制必須明寫，求解器沒有其他方法知道。

06Decision support systems決策支援系統決策支援系統Decision support systems

Intelligence情報→ Design設計→ Choice選擇
  • Herbert Simon's three stages. Intelligence is the most important: define the wrong problem and the whole effort is wasted. Symptoms are not problems.Herbert Simon 的三階段。情報階段最重要：定義錯問題，整個努力就白費。症狀不是問題。
  • A DSS is a CBIS focused on decision-making effectiveness, mostly for mid and top management, useful for semi-structured and unstructured problems.DSS 是專注決策效能的 CBIS，主要供中高層管理使用，適合半結構化與非結構化問題。
  • Optimising finds the best solution under the constraints; satisficing accepts one good enough.最優化在限制下求最佳解；滿意化接受足夠好的解。
Six characteristics六項特徵

Predictive · Summary · Ad hoc · Unexpected · External · Subjective.
Hook: PSAUES.預測性・彙總・臨時・非預期・外部・主觀。
口訣：PSAUES。

Predictive = outputs concern the future. Summary = global, not line-by-line. Ad hoc = produced irregularly for one purpose. Unexpected = forecasts surprise managers. External = inputs from outside the firm. Subjective = personal opinion, accuracy suspect.預測性＝輸出關於未來。彙總＝整體而非逐筆。臨時＝為特定目的不定期產生。非預期＝預測常令管理者意外。外部＝輸入來自公司以外。主觀＝個人意見，準確度可議。

07Transaction processing交易處理系統交易處理系統Transaction processing

  • A transaction is any business exchange. A TPS records completed transactions — the earliest use of computers, and the data foundation a DSS later mines.交易是任何業務往來。TPS 記錄已完成的交易——最早使用電腦的地方，也是日後 DSS 挖掘資料的基礎。
  • Benefits: lower cost, more speed, more accuracy, better customer service, more data for decisions.效益：降低成本、提升速度、提升準確度、改善客戶服務、為決策提供更多資料。
Batch批次 On-line (OLTP)線上（OLTP）
Transactions accumulate, then run as one unit — there is a delay. Payroll, monthly billing, end-of-day reconciliation.交易先累積，再一次處理——存在延誤。薪酬、月結帳單、日終對帳。 Each transaction is processed and records updated at once. Airline seats, stock trades, online betting, ATM.每筆交易即時處理、相關紀錄同步更新。機位、股票買賣、網上投注、自動櫃員機。
Two sixes — do not mix them up兩個「六」——不要混淆

Six activities: collection · editing · correction · manipulation · storage · document / report production.六項活動：收集・編輯・修正・處理・儲存・文件／報表產生。

Six characteristics: repetitive · predictable · emphasis on the past · detailed · structured · great accuracy.六項特徵：重複・可預測・著重過去・詳細・結構化・高度準確。

08E-commerce電子商務電子商務E-commerce

  • An electronic marketplace links buyers and sellers over a network; the Internet is the major factor — a “network of networks” of more than 25,000 networks.電子市場透過網絡連接買賣雙方；互聯網是主要因素——由超過 25,000 個網絡組成的「網絡的網絡」。
  • Six reasons firms adopt it: globalisation · competitive advantage · information access · cost containment · sales and marketing · electronic communications.企業採用的六個理由：全球化・競爭優勢・資訊取得・成本控制・銷售與市場推廣・電子通訊。
Form形式 Who is involved參與者 Example例子
B2Cbusiness → consumer, no middle-man企業 → 消費者，無中間人 Amazon.com; Dell selling you a computerAmazon.com；Dell 直接賣電腦給你
B2Borganisation → organisation (both sides are firms)組織 → 組織（雙方都是企業） Intel selling CPUs to Dell; Heinz selling ketchup to McDonald'sIntel 賣 CPU 給 Dell；Heinz 賣番茄醬給麥當勞
C2Cconsumer → consumer, individuals on both sides消費者 → 消費者，雙方都是個人 eBay — second-hand items sold directly between userseBay——使用者之間直接買賣二手物品

B2B means both sides are organisations, not “business products”. A chip that later reaches a consumer still travels through a B2B transaction.B2B 指雙方都是組織，不是「商業產品」。晶片日後進入消費市場，該筆交易仍是 B2B。

IF has exactly three partsIF 正好有三個部分

=IF( condition , value if TRUE , value if FALSE )
=IF(B3>=50,"Pass","Fail")
  • Text results go in double quotes; numbers, cell references and other formulas do not. Operators: = <> > < >= <=.文字結果要加雙引號；數字、儲存格參照與其他公式不加。運算子：= <> > < >= <=。
  • Nested IF puts a whole IF inside one of the three parts — usually the FALSE part, where “everything else” lives.巢狀 IF 把一整個 IF 放進三個部分的其中一個——通常在 FALSE 部分，即「其餘所有情況」。
=IF(B3>=80,"A",IF(B3>=65,"B",IF(B3>=50,"C","F")))
Trap易錯位

Count the IFs and count the closing brackets — they must be equal. A missing bracket is the most common lost mark in this section.數一數 IF 的數目，再數右括號的數目——必須相等。漏括號是這部分最常見的失分原因。

Worth memorising: SUM AVERAGE MAX MIN COUNT COUNTA COUNTIF SUMIF ROUND.要記住：SUM AVERAGE MAX MIN COUNT COUNTA COUNTIF SUMIF ROUND。

10Cram sheet考前速記考前速記Cram sheet

Ten lines that between them cover most of the paper. Read them once before you go in.以下十句已覆蓋大部分考卷。入場前讀一遍。

  • A calculation across records makes it information; a raw fact is data.跨紀錄計算得出的是資訊；原始事實是數據。
  • Efficiency measures the technology, effectiveness measures the business goal.效率量度科技，效能量度業務目標。
  • Programming is stage 4; the DFD is stage 3; feasibility is stage 1.程式編寫是第 4 階段；DFD 是第 3 階段；可行性研究是第 1 階段。
  • Malware is exactly four things: virus, trojan, spyware, adware.惡意軟件正好四種：病毒、木馬、間諜軟件、廣告軟件。
  • Public encrypts, private decrypts; a CA vouches for the public key.公鑰加密、私鑰解密；CA 為公鑰背書。
  • Authentication first (who), authorisation second (what).先認證（你是誰），後授權（可做甚麼）。
  • Solver = variables + constraints + objective, and everything is a number.求解器＝變數＋限制＋目標，全部是數字。
  • Intelligence is the most important of Simon's three stages.Simon 三階段中，情報最重要。
  • TPS output is detailed and past-facing; DSS output is summarised and forward-looking.TPS 輸出具體且面向過去；DSS 輸出彙總且面向未來。
  • $ freezes what follows it; two dollars freeze the whole address.一個 $ 凍結其後那一個元素；兩個 $ 凍結整個位址。
